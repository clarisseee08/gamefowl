# Photograph credits

Licensed stock photographs, used for the masthead and the section bands.

**They are not records of birds this farm owns.** Every bird in the catalogue,
in the featured strip and on a bird's own page is the farm's own photograph,
served from the farm's own storage. The stock images below are atmosphere —
they carry a heading, never a price or a band number — and none is captioned
or framed as a particular bird. Keep it that way: a stock bird placed where
stock is listed is a picture of an animal nobody can sell.

Pexels does not require attribution. This file exists so the farm can answer
where any image on its site came from.
